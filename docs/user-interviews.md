# Kheyal — Student User Interview Guides

Standardized interview scripts for the 14-day student validation pilot.

---

## Interviewer Guidelines

- **Duration:** 10–15 minutes per session. Conducted via voice call or video screen-share.
- **Mindset:** Listen 80%, speak 20%. Never defend design choices; capture raw student perception.
- **Recording:** Take verbatim quotes. Record specific dates, subjects, and actions mentioned.

---

## Session 1: Day 3 — First Impressions & Capture Friction

**Objective:** Verify that single-line capture and the preview card feel effortless and intuitive.

### Questions
1. "How many deadlines have you added so far? What subjects were they for?"
2. "When you typed your deadline in the box (e.g. `DBMS Assignment by Friday 5 PM ~3h`), did the preview card understand what you meant on the first try?"
   - *Probe:* "Was anything parsed incorrectly (date, time, or hours)?"
   - *Probe:* "Did you edit any fields on the preview card before confirming?"
3. "Did you notice the suggested start-by time on the 'Do this next' card? What did you think of it?"
4. "Was there any moment you felt confused or wondered what to click next?"
5. "If you had to rate the speed of adding a deadline from 1 (painful) to 5 (instant), what would you give it?"

---

## Session 2: Day 7 — Mid-Point Pulse & Notification Experience

**Objective:** Verify that push reminders arrive reliably, the copy feels respectful, and slack prioritization reduces stress.

### Questions
1. "Have you received any push notifications from Kheyal on your phone or laptop this week?"
   - *Probe:* "Did any alert arrive at a bad time (e.g. late night during sleep)?"
   - *Probe:* "How did the notification text feel? Did it cause panic, or did it feel like a helpful nudge?"
2. "When you opened the app with multiple deadlines pending, did the #1 'Do this next' task match what you actually needed to work on first?"
   - *Probe:* "Did the slack reasoning (e.g. 'Only 2h slack remaining') make sense to you?"
3. "Did you mark any deadline as complete? Did you notice the 'Undo' option?"
4. "Have you installed the app on your home screen, or are you using it in a browser tab?"
5. "What has been the most useful part of Kheyal so far this week?"

---

## Session 3: Day 14 — Exit Interview & Impact Evaluation

**Objective:** Measure overall impact on student panic, follow-through rate, and willingness to retain Kheyal as their primary workspace.

### Questions
1. **Stress & Panic:**
   - "Looking back over the last two weeks, did Kheyal help prevent any last-minute scramble or forgotten submissions?"
   - "Did you feel more in control of your deadlines compared to previous weeks?"
2. **Feature Comparison:**
   - "How does Kheyal compare to whatever you were using before (Notion, Google Calendar, WhatsApp self-chat, paper notes)?"
   - "What does Kheyal do better? What does your old tool do that you miss in Kheyal?"
3. **Missing Needs & Friction:**
   - "Were there any deadlines you wanted to add but couldn't because of the format?"
   - "Did you ever feel annoyed by a reminder?"
4. **Retention & Advocacy:**
   - "If Kheyal was taken away tomorrow, would you feel disappointed?"
     - *Options:* Very disappointed / Somewhat disappointed / Not disappointed.
   - "Will you continue using Kheyal for the rest of your semester?"
   - "Would you recommend Kheyal to your classmates? How would you describe it to them?"
5. **Open Feedback:**
   - "If you had a magic wand and could change one thing in Kheyal, what would it be?"
